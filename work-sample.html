What it does

Measuring what contractors lose
before selling them a solution.

Most trade businesses lose time and margin not from lack of skill, but from operational debt that no one has ever had the bandwidth to address. Forma surfaces that debt in numbers.

A 5-minute intake form collects quoting volume, admin hours, margin tracking habits, and web visibility. An AI scores each dimension against regional and trade-specific benchmarks. The output is a PDF report that names the problem, quantifies the cost, and locks the solution behind a call — creating the commercial tension that drives conversion.

Key finding

What a year of running this
taught me about the segment.

Finding 01 — Activation

"Contractors don't self-serve into calls. Zero bookings through scheduling links. Outbound call within 24 hours: the only conversion lever."

I tested Calendly as the primary booking mechanism for the debrief call. Click-through was near zero. These are people who run physical businesses, manage their week verbally, and don't navigate browser-based scheduling flows. The activation has to come from you — every time. Any onboarding sequence that relies on self-initiated digital steps will systematically underperform on this segment.

Finding 02 — Conversion

"The question that unlocks the sale is never about software. It's about time and money — anchored in their own numbers."

The diagnostic form collects admin hours and billable rate. The opening line of every call: "You told me 12 hours a week on admin. At your rate, that's roughly €500 a week. What would it be worth to recover six of those?" Once the cost of the current state is anchored in their own data, the price of the solution becomes a footnote. The report creates the urgency. The call closes it.
